09 · Methodology

Methodology

The project follows a transparent actuarial valuation chain rather than treating the final premium as a black box.

01 · Mortality

Makeham mortality produces qx values. The mortality multiplier changes the mortality basis used in the calculations.

02 · Life table

lx is generated recursively, giving survival and deferred-death probabilities.

03 · EPV

Future benefits are discounted using the selected effective annual interest rate.

04 · Net premium

The equivalence principle balances the EPV of benefits and the EPV of premiums.

05 · Gross premium

Expenses, commissions and profit/risk loading are layered on top of the net premium.

06 · Reserve

Prospective reserve represents future benefit value less future premium value at the selected duration.

Decision rules used in this portfolio model: green when loading and stress movement are relatively contained; amber when review is warranted; red when the selected assumptions create a comparatively high pricing/reserve burden. These are illustrative decision rules, not production governance thresholds.

Limitations

Illustrative Makeham mortality basis; no smoker/sex/select distinction, lapse or surrender, underwriting classes, tax, regulatory capital, or full profit test. The model is designed to demonstrate actuarial reasoning and sensitivity, not production pricing.
